# Proposal Deck (Pre-Sales)

> **Purpose**: Formal project proposal after quotation acceptance  
> **Format**: 10-slide deck (PDF or Google Slides)  
> **When**: After client shows interest in ballpark quotation (M00.5)  
> **Target**: Medium/Large projects

---

## How to Create This Deck

### Option 1: Google Slides (Recommended)
1. Use this template as outline
2. Add your branding (logo, colors)
3. Keep visuals simple (charts, icons)
4. Export as PDF before sending

### Option 2: PowerPoint
- Similar approach to Google Slides
- Export as PDF (not .pptx - easier to view)

### Option 3: Notion/Markdown → PDF
- Write in Notion or markdown
- Use tool like Notion export or pandoc
- More text-heavy, less visual

**Recommended**: Google Slides (easier to present on call)

---

## Design Tips

**Keep It Simple**:
- ✅ One main point per slide
- ✅ Large fonts (min 18pt for body text)
- ✅ Lots of white space
- ❌ Don't cram information
- ❌ Don't use Comic Sans

**Visuals**:
- Use icons (flaticon.com, noun project)
- Simple charts (bar, timeline)
- Screenshots of similar work (if allowed)
- Stock photos only if high quality

**Branding**:
- Your logo on every slide (corner)
- Consistent color scheme
- Professional fonts (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans)

## Presentation Script (30 min call)

**Introduction (2 min)**:
- "Thanks for your time today"
- "I'll walk through our proposal"
- "Feel free to interrupt with questions"

**Slides 1-3 (5 min)**:
- Recap discovery call
- Confirm understanding of problem
- Show before/after vision

**Slides 4-6 (10 min)**:
- Detailed scope walkthrough
- Technical approach (keep high-level)
- Answer "how" questions

**Slides 7-8 (8 min)**:
- Timeline expectations
- Payment structure
- Set milestone expectations

**Slide 9 (3 min)**:
- Social proof (past projects)
- Build trust

**Slide 10 (2 min)**:
- Next steps
- Clear CTA

**Q&A (remaining time)**

## Common Objections & Responses

### "The price is too high"
**Response**: "I understand. Let's look at the ROI - you're currently spending 15 hours/week on manual work. At Rp 50K/hour labor cost, that's Rp 3M/month. This system pays for itself in 2 years, plus you gain accuracy and speed."

**Alternative**: "We can start with an MVP (core P0 features only), then add advanced features later via Change Request."

### "The timeline is too long"
**Response**: "8 weeks ensures quality and proper testing. We can compress to 6 weeks if you can commit to 24-hour feedback turnaround. Faster than 6 weeks risks quality issues."

### "Can you start before payment?"
**Response**: "Standard practice is DP before work starts. This protects both of us - ensures you're committed, and I can dedicate resources to your project."

### "We're talking to other developers"
**Response**: "That's smart! When comparing proposals, check:
- Do they understand your problem?
- Do they have relevant experience?
- Is the timeline realistic?
- What's included in warranty?

I'm confident our approach stands up to comparison."

### "We need to think about it"
**Response**: "Absolutely. What specific concerns can I address to help your decision? Timeline? Budget? Technical approach?"

(Uncover real objection, don't let it be vague)

## After the Presentation

### If Client Says Yes
1. Send contract for signature
2. Send invoice for DP
3. Schedule kickoff call (Week 1)
4. Start work after DP received

### If Client Needs Time
1. Send follow-up email with deck PDF
2. Set follow-up date ("I'll check in Friday?")
3. Don't be pushy, but don't ghost

### If Client Says No
1. Ask why (learn for next time)
2. "Can I follow up in 3 months if timing changes?"
3. Stay professional, maintain relationship
