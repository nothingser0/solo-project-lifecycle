# Discovery Call Checklist (Pre-Sales)

> **Purpose**: Qualify leads and gather requirements during initial sales call  
> **Duration**: 30-60 minutes  
> **When**: Before sending proposal (M00.5 Pre-Sales)  
> **Target**: Medium/Large projects (Agency/Vendor sales)

---

## Pre-Call Preparation

- [ ] **Research client**: Check website, LinkedIn, industry
- [ ] **Prepare questions**: Print this checklist
- [ ] **Set expectations**: "30-minute discovery call to understand your needs"
- [ ] **Recording consent**: "May I record this for notes?" (optional)

---

**Listen for**:

### 4. Budget Range
**Question**: "Do you have a budget range in mind for this project?"

**Listen for**:
- Specific number vs "whatever it takes"
- Budget authority (decision maker vs researcher)
- Payment flexibility

**Responses**:
- If they give range: "That falls into [Small/Medium/Large] scale, which typically includes..."
- If they say "depends on scope": "Typically projects like this range from Rp X to Y. Does that align?"
- If they say "as cheap as possible": 🚩 Low-budget client, qualify carefully

## BANT Qualification

Score each criterion:

### Budget
- [ ] **Strong**: Clear budget, aligns with scope (3 points)
- [ ] **Medium**: Budget exists but tight (2 points)
- [ ] **Weak**: No budget or unrealistic expectations (1 point)

### Authority
- [ ] **Strong**: Speaking with decision maker (3 points)
- [ ] **Medium**: Influencer, needs approval (2 points)
- [ ] **Weak**: Researcher, no authority (1 point)

### Need
- [ ] **Strong**: Urgent, clear pain point (3 points)
- [ ] **Medium**: Nice to have, not urgent (2 points)
- [ ] **Weak**: Vague, exploring options (1 point)

### Timeline
- [ ] **Strong**: Realistic timeline, ready to start (3 points)
- [ ] **Medium**: Flexible timeline (2 points)
- [ ] **Weak**: Unrealistic or no urgency (1 point)

**Total Score**: _____ / 12

**Decision**:
- **9-12 points**: 🟢 Qualified lead → Send proposal
- **6-8 points**: 🟡 Maybe → Follow up, nurture
- **<6 points**: 🔴 Not qualified → Politely decline or defer

## Red Flags Checklist

**Stop and reconsider if client**:
- [ ] Requests free work upfront ("build a demo first")
- [ ] Compares you to $5 Fiverr developers
- [ ] Has fired 3+ previous developers
- [ ] Cannot explain what they want
- [ ] Refuses to discuss budget at all
- [ ] Wants "Facebook but for [niche]" in 2 weeks
- [ ] Says "I have the next billion-dollar idea"
- [ ] Demands NDA before discussing project (for standard work)
- [ ] Pushes for immediate start without scope discussion

**If 2+ red flags**: Politely decline or quote 2-3x normal rate.

## Post-Call Actions

Within 24 hours:
- [ ] Send follow-up email summarizing discussion
- [ ] Attach ballpark estimate (T-shirt sizing: S/M/L/XL)
- [ ] Schedule proposal presentation call (if qualified)
- [ ] Update CRM with notes and BANT score

**Email Template**:
```
Subject: Follow-up - [Project Name] Discovery Call

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for the call today! Here's a quick summary:

Problem: [1-sentence summary]
Goal: [success criteria]
Timeline: [target date]
Budget range: [if discussed]

Based on our discussion, this project falls into [Scale] category, 
typically Rp X - Y and Z weeks timeline.

Next steps:
1. I'll prepare a detailed proposal by [date]
2. Let's schedule 30 min to walk through it together
3. Available slots: [provide 3 options]

Questions? Reply to this email or WhatsApp [phone].

Best,
[Your name]
```
