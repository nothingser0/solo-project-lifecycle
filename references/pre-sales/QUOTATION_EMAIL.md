## For Developer: How to Use This Template

### Step 1: Gather Info from Discovery Call
Use `DISCOVERY_CALL_CHECKLIST.md` notes:
- Features discussed
- Budget mentioned (if any)
- Timeline expectations
- Pain points

### Step 2: Calculate Ballpark

**Quick Formula**:
```
Hours Estimate = (Features × Complexity × Buffer)
Budget = Hours × Hourly Rate
Timeline = Hours / (Team Size × 40 hours/week)
```

**T-Shirt Sizing**:
- **Small**: Rp 20-50M, 1-3 months, 1-2 people
- **Medium**: Rp 50-200M, 3-6 months, 3-6 people
- **Large**: Rp 200-500M, 6-12 months, 6-12 people
- **Enterprise**: >Rp 500M, 12-24 months, 12+ people

### Step 3: Fill Template
- Replace all `[placeholders]`
- Be honest with ranges (don't lowball to win)
- Include what's NOT included (prevents scope creep)

### Step 4: Send Within 24 Hours
- Speed shows professionalism
- Strike while interest is hot
- Delays = client moves to competitors

### Step 5: Follow Up
- If no reply in 3 days: "Just following up on the estimate I sent..."
- If they ghost after 2 follow-ups: Move on, they're not ready

## When to Skip This Template

**Skip if**:
- Lead is clearly not qualified (BANT score <6)
- Budget way too low (<Rp 10M for complex project)
- Timeline impossible (full CRM in 2 weeks)
- Red flags in discovery call (fired 3 devs, unrealistic)

**Send polite decline instead**:
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out. Based on our discussion, this project requires 
[X timeline/budget] to do properly. If that doesn't align with your current 
plans, I recommend [alternative: freelancer platform for smaller budget / 
starting with MVP].

Happy to revisit if your timeline/budget changes!

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Integration with Workflow

**Pre-Sales Flow**:
1. Lead inquiry
2. Discovery call (use `DISCOVERY_CALL_CHECKLIST.md`)
3. **Send quotation email** (this template) within 24h

5. If client accepts → Contract + DP → Start M02 (Discovery & Scope)

**Conversion Rate**:
- 10 discovery calls → 7 quotations sent → 3 proposals → 1 signed contract
