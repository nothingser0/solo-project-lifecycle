# Enterprise Growth Engineering & Marketing Intelligence Pattern

> **Module**: M00 (Product Discovery Strategy), M01 (Idea Feasibility), & M13 (Product Operations & Iteration)  
> **Source**: Adapted from a published marketing-skills-for-agents library.  
> **Scope**: Growth engineering frameworks, conversion rate optimization (CRO) across landing pages/signup/onboarding/paywalls, rigorous copywriting and email lifecycle flows, technical & AI search optimization (SEO, AEO, GEO), analytics tracking setup, churn prevention, and product marketing context dependency mapping.

---

## 1. Product Marketing Foundation & Cross-Referencing Architecture
Every marketing task builds on shared foundational context. The `product-marketing` skill acts as the root dependency—all other growth and copywriting skills inspect it first to understand product positioning, audience personas, and value propositions before executing.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Product Marketing Context (product-marketing)               │
│ (Read by all other marketing skills first)                  │
└──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                               │
            ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
            ▼                  ▼                  ▼
┌──────────────────────┐ ┌───────────────┐ ┌──────────────────┐
│ Conversion & CRO     │ │ Copywriting   │ │ SEO & AI Search  │
│ (Signup, Paywalls)   │ │ (Emails, Ads) │ │ (AEO, GEO, Schema)│
└──────────────────────% └───────────────┘ └──────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Core Growth Engineering Principles
1. **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: Systematically optimize pages, registration flows, onboarding sequences, popups, and in-app paywall upgrade moments.
2. **Lifecycle & Content Copywriting**: Execute data-informed marketing copy, cold outbound email sequences, automated lifecycle drip flows, and direct-response advertising creative.
3. **Multi-Channel Discovery & Measurement**: Combine technical/on-page SEO with AI search optimization (AEO, GEO, LLMO), programmatic SEO, and robust analytics event tracking setup.
4. **Retention & Monetization Engineering**: Deploy churn-prevention flows, cancel recovery offers, referral/affiliate programs, and pricing/packaging optimization models.
