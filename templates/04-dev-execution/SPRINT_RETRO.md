## How to Run Sprint Retrospective

### Before Meeting (15 min prep)
- [ ] Facilitator reviews sprint metrics
- [ ] Prepare template (this document or whiteboard)
- [ ] Set timer for meeting (max 60 minutes)

### During Meeting (45-60 min)

**Part 1: Set the Stage (5 min)**
- Welcome team
- Remind: "Safe space, no blame, focus on improvement"
- Review sprint metrics

**Part 2: Gather Data (15 min)**
- What went well? (each person shares)
- What didn't go well? (each person shares)
- Use Start/Stop/Continue framework

**Part 3: Generate Insights (15 min)**
- Group similar themes
- Vote on top 3 issues to address
- Deep dive on root causes

**Part 4: Decide Actions (10 min)**
- For each issue, decide action
- Assign owner and deadline
- Make it SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

**Part 5: Close (5 min)**
- Recap action items
- Shout-outs
- Mood check

### After Meeting
- [ ] Document action items
- [ ] Share retro notes with team
- [ ] Add action items to next sprint planning
- [ ] Follow up on action items mid-sprint

## Retrospective Formats (Alternatives)

### Format 1: Start/Stop/Continue (This Template)
**Best for**: Standard teams, balanced feedback

### Format 2: Mad/Sad/Glad
- **Mad**: What made you angry/frustrated?
- **Sad**: What disappointed you?
- **Glad**: What made you happy?

**Best for**: Emotional debrief after tough sprint

### Format 3: 4Ls
- **Liked**: What did you enjoy?
- **Learned**: What did you discover?
- **Lacked**: What was missing?
- **Longed For**: What do you wish we had?

**Best for**: Learning-focused teams

### Format 4: Sailboat
- **Wind** (propelling us): What helped us move forward?
- **Anchor** (holding us back): What slowed us down?
- **Rocks** (risks ahead): What obstacles do we see?
- **Island** (goal): Where are we heading?

**Best for**: Visual teams, metaphor lovers

## Common Retrospective Anti-Patterns

❌ **Blame Game**: "John's code review was too slow"  
✅ **Process Focus**: "Code review SLA needs definition"

❌ **No Action Items**: Talk but no follow-up  
✅ **Commit to 1-3 Actions**: Track and verify next sprint

❌ **Same Issues Repeatedly**: Complain, never fix  
✅ **Root Cause Analysis**: Why is this happening? How to prevent?

❌ **Manager Dominates**: Team silent, manager lectures  
✅ **Team Driven**: Everyone participates, facilitator guides

❌ **Skip Retro**: "Too busy, no time"  
✅ **Protect the Time**: Non-negotiable, mandatory improvement

3. Reduce meetings: Bundle client calls into 1/week (Owner: Sarah, Due: Next week)

Shout-Outs:

## Integration with Workflow

**Sprint Cycle**:
1. **Day 1**: Sprint planning
2. **Day 1-10**: Daily standup + development
3. **Day 10**: Sprint demo (show client)
4. **Day 10**: **Sprint retrospective** (this meeting)
5. **Day 11**: Next sprint planning (apply retro learnings)

**Retro Frequency**:
- Every 2 weeks (end of sprint)
- Special retro after major incident/milestone
- Skip ONLY if entire team unavailable (rare)

- [ ] Everyone participates

- [ ] Notes documented
- [ ] Action items tracked
