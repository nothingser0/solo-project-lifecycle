# Enterprise Architecture & Technical Advisory Agreement

> **Commercial Contract Template**: Specifically designed for Independent Technical Consultants / Lead Architects providing enterprise governance, C4 architectural blueprints, procurement readiness, and compliance advisory.
> **Key Protections**: Strictly separates advisory deliverables from coding/implementation liabilities, incorporates Net 30/60 corporate terms, strict liability caps, and integrity/anti-conflict declarations.

---

## 1. Parties & Engagement Premise

This Advisory Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on [YYYY-MM-DD] by and between:
1. **CLIENT**: [Corporate / Enterprise Client Name], located at [Official Corporate Address].
2. **CONSULTANT**: [Your Name / Consultancy Entity], Independent Enterprise Software Architect.

### 1.1 Nature of Engagement (Pure Advisory)
The Consultant is engaged solely in an **architectural advisory and technical governance capacity**. The Consultant **DOES NOT PROVIDE HANDS-ON SOFTWARE PROGRAMMING, CODE WRITING, OR DIRECT OPERATIONAL SERVER HOSTING** under this Agreement. Implementation of software shall be performed by third-party engineering vendors or Client internal staff.

---

## 2. Advisory Deliverables & Scope (The A-Series Package)

The Consultant shall deliver the verified **Enterprise Readiness & Architecture Package**:
1. **Phase A00**: Pre-Sales / Bid-No-Bid Evaluation Report & Administrative Eligibility Checklist.
2. **Phase A01**: Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) & Phased Decomposition Plan (splitting system into autonomous Medium-scale sub-projects).
3. **Phase A02**: C4 Architecture Blueprint, Threat Model (STRIDE), and Non-Functional Quality Scenarios.
4. **Phase A03**: Vendor Procurement Specifications (RFP Technical Schedule & Weighted Comparison Matrix).
5. **Phase A04**: Governance Handover (RACI Matrix, CAB Protocol, and Architecture Conformance Review Retainer).

---

## 3. Professional Fees, Invoicing & Corporate Net Payment Terms

1. **Total Advisory Fee**: Rp [Amount] / USD [Amount] (Exclusive of statutory VAT/PPN).
2. **Milestone Schedule**:
   - **Retainer / Kickoff (30%)**: Due upon signing, prior to Phase A01 commencement.
   - **Architecture & Threat Model Sign-off (40%)**: Due upon delivery and review of Phase A02.
   - **Procurement & Governance Final Handover (30%)**: Due upon delivery of Phase A03 & A04.
3. **Corporate Invoicing Terms (Net 30)**:
   - Invoices shall be payable within **30 (thirty) calendar days** of issuance.
   - Late payments exceeding 14 calendar days past due date incur interest of 1% per month.
   - Work on subsequent phases shall pause automatically if prior milestone payment is overdue.

---

## 4. Integrity Pact, Anti-Bribery & Conflict of Interest

1. **Anti-Corruption Compliance**: Both parties pledge zero tolerance for bribery, gratification, kickbacks, or illicit commissions under Indonesian Anti-Corruption Law (UU Tipikor) and international anti-bribery standards (FCPA / UK Bribery Act).
2. **Vendor Objectivity (Anti-Conflict Clause)**: The Consultant warrants that they have no commercial ties, affiliate commissions, or equity interest in third-party engineering vendors being evaluated under Phase A03. The Consultant shall not bid on, nor accept subcontracts under, engineering vendors selected to implement this architecture.

---

## 5. Professional Liability Limitation & Disclaimer

1. **Disclaimer of Implementation Liabilities**: The Consultant provides architectural recommendations based on current technical and regulatory best practices. The Consultant shall not be liable for coding defects, runtime system failures, security breaches arising from vendor implementation flaws, or data loss caused by third-party software.
2. **Maximum Liability Cap**: The maximum total liability of the Consultant to the Client under any cause of action whatsoever is strictly capped at **the total advisory fees actually received by the Consultant under this Agreement**.
3. **Legal & Compliance Status**: All architecture blueprints, regulatory compliance matrices (UU PDP, OJK, BI), and contractual drafts generated under this advisory constitute **technical engineering recommendations and MUST BE FORMALLY REVIEWED BY LICENSED LEGAL COUNSEL** before corporate execution.
