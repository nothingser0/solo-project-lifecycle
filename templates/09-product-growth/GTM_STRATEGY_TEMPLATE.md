## 4. Launch Channels & Budget Allocation

### Channel Mix (Recommended: Focus on 3-5 channels max)

| Channel | Tactic | Budget | Expected Reach | Priority |
|---------|--------|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Product Hunt** | Launch on Tuesday, hunter outreach | $0-200 (ads optional) | 500-2,000 upvotes | HIGH |
| **Social Media (Twitter/X)** | Launch thread, daily tips, engage in #buildinpublic | $0 (organic) | 1,000-5,000 impressions | HIGH |
| **Content Marketing (Blog/SEO)** | 5 pillar articles (how-to, guides) | $0 (DIY) | 500-2,000 organic visits/month | MEDIUM |
| **Community Marketing** | Post in Telegram/Reddit groups, answer questions | $0 | 200-1,000 targeted users | HIGH |
| **Paid Ads (Google/Meta)** | Search ads for high-intent keywords | Rp1-3 million/month | 50-200 signups | LOW (test later) |
| **Email Outreach** | Cold email to niche newsletters, podcast sponsors | $0 | 10-50 partnerships | MEDIUM |
| **Influencer/Affiliate** | Micro-influencers (500-10k followers) in niche | $0-500 (barter/commission) | 500-3,000 impressions | MEDIUM |

**Total Budget:** [Total across all channels]

**Channel Selection Criteria:**
- **Owned (free):** Blog, social media, email list — build long-term asset
- **Earned (free):** PR, word-of-mouth, reviews — credibility boost
- **Paid (cost):** Ads, sponsorships — only AFTER organic validation

**Budget Allocation Rule:** 80% time on organic (owned+earned), 20% budget on paid (only after PMF)

## 5. Pre-Launch (4-8 Weeks Before Launch)

### Goals:
- Build waiting list: [Target number, e.g., 200-500 signups]
- Create launch assets (landing page, demo video, screenshots)
- Secure 5-10 beta users for testimonials

### Tactics:

**Week -8 to -6: Build Audience**
- [ ] Launch simple landing page with email signup (use Carrd/Typedream)
- [ ] Start posting on Twitter: problem tweets, build-in-public updates (3x/week)
- [ ] Join 5-10 relevant communities (introduce yourself, don't pitch yet)
- [ ] Reach out to 20 potential beta users (offer free lifetime access)

**Week -6 to -4: Beta Testing**
- [ ] Onboard 5-10 beta users
- [ ] Collect testimonials (ask: "What's the #1 benefit you got?")
- [ ] Record demo video (2-3 minutes, show problem → solution → result)
- [ ] Prepare launch assets:
  - [ ] Screenshots (5-7 key screens)
  - [ ] Social media graphics (og:image, Twitter card)
  - [ ] Press kit (logo, founder bio, boilerplate description)

**Week -4 to -2: Content Creation**
- [ ] Write 3 launch-related blog posts:
  1. Origin story ("Why I built this")
  2. Problem deep-dive ("The hidden cost of [problem]")
  3. How-to guide ("How to [achieve outcome] in 5 steps")
- [ ] Create launch announcement thread (Twitter, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Draft Product Hunt description + tagline

**Week -2 to Launch Day: Outreach**
- [ ] Email waiting list: "We launch in 2 weeks — here's early access"
- [ ] Reach out to 10 micro-influencers / newsletter owners for launch day mention
- [ ] Schedule launch day posts (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, Reddit)
- [ ] Recruit Product Hunt hunter (check leaderboard, DM top 50 hunters)

## 6. Launch Day Playbook

**Platform:** Product Hunt (recommended: launch on Tuesday 00:01 PST)

**Hour-by-Hour Schedule:**

**00:00-02:00 (Launch Moment)**
- [ ] Publish Product Hunt listing (tagline, gallery, first comment)
- [ ] Post launch thread on Twitter/X with demo video
- [ ] Post in LinkedIn with personal story
- [ ] Email waiting list with launch link + ask for upvote
- [ ] Notify beta users & ask for reviews

**02:00-08:00 (Morning Push)**
- [ ] Monitor Product Hunt comments → respond within 15 min
- [ ] Share in 5-10 communities (Reddit, Telegram, Discord)
- [ ] Post in Facebook groups (avoid spammy, add value first)
- [ ] Engage with every Twitter mention/reply

**08:00-18:00 (Peak Hours)**
- [ ] Keep replying to PH comments (response rate matters for ranking)
- [ ] Share milestones on Twitter ("We just hit 100 upvotes! 🚀")
- [ ] Go live on Twitter Spaces / Instagram Live (optional)
- [ ] Reach out to journalists/bloggers covering your niche

**18:00-24:00 (Final Push)**
- [ ] Post in Asian time zone communities (if global product)
- [ ] Send "last chance" email to waiting list non-voters
- [ ] Celebrate on social media regardless of ranking

**Success Metrics (Product Hunt):**
- Top 5 of the day: Excellent (500-1,000+ upvotes)
- Top 10 of the day: Good (200-500 upvotes)
- Top 20 of the day: Decent (100-200 upvotes)

- [ ] Reply to every feedback email/tweet within 24h
- [ ] Post 1-2 updates on social media (user testimonials, quick wins)

**Content to Publish:**
- [ ] Launch recap blog post ("We launched on PH — here's what happened")
- [ ] User success story (interview 1 early user)
- [ ] Twitter thread: "5 things I learned from our launch"

## 8. Month 1-3 Post-Launch (Growth Phase)

### Growth Tactics by Channel:

**Content Marketing (SEO)**
- Publish 2-4 blog posts/month targeting long-tail keywords
- Optimize for featured snippets (use structured data)
- Build backlinks: guest posts, directory submissions, HARO

**Community Marketing**
- Answer 5-10 questions/week in Quora, Reddit, forums (add value, not pitch)
- Host AMA (Ask Me Anything) in relevant communities
- Partner with 1-2 communities for sponsored posts

**Social Media (Organic)**
- Twitter: 1 educational thread/week + daily tips
- LinkedIn: 1 long-form post/week (case studies, insights)
- Instagram/TikTok (if B2C): Short-form video tips

**Email Marketing**
- Send weekly newsletter (mix: product updates + educational content)
- Segment users: active vs churned vs trial → targeted campaigns
- Run re-engagement campaign for inactive users (discount/incentive)

**Paid Acquisition (Once Validated)**
- Start with $50-100/week Google Search ads (high-intent keywords)
- A/B test landing page copy & CTA
- Track CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) → aim for CAC < 3x LTV

## 11. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Low Product Hunt traction | Medium | Medium | Pre-build waiting list 200+, recruit hunter early |
| No press coverage | High | Low | Focus on organic community, don't rely on press |
| High CAC (paid ads unprofitable) | Medium | High | Start organic-only, validate PMF before paid |
| Competitor launches similar product | Low | Medium | Differentiate on UX + niche focus (freelancers not enterprise) |
| Technical issues on launch day | Low | High | Load test, have rollback plan, monitor uptime |

## 12. Launch Checklist (Final 24 Hours)

**Technical:**
- [ ] Load testing (simulate 100 concurrent users)
- [ ] SSL certificate valid
- [ ] Error monitoring active (Sentry)
- [ ] Analytics tracking live (Mixpanel/GA4)
- [ ] Backup of production DB

**Content:**
- [ ] Landing page copy reviewed (no typos)
- [ ] Demo video embedded & tested
- [ ] Pricing page clear & accurate
- [ ] FAQ page covers top 10 questions
- [ ] Legal pages live (Privacy Policy, Terms)

**Marketing:**
- [ ] Social media posts scheduled
- [ ] Email to waiting list ready (send 1 hour before launch)
- [ ] Product Hunt description finalized
- [ ] Press kit uploaded (Dropbox/Google Drive link)
- [ ] Testimonials/social proof on landing page

**Support:**
- [ ] Support email monitored (set up auto-reply)
- [ ] Live chat widget installed (optional)
- [ ] FAQ/docs link prominent in app
