# SaaS Pricing, Packaging & Monetization Guide

> **Module**: M01 (Idea & Business Model) & M13 (Product Operations & Continuous Iteration)  
> **Source**: Adapted from value-based SaaS pricing frameworks (a published marketing-skills-for-agents library).  
> **Objective**: Eliminate underpricing traps, select sustainable value metrics, and execute de-risked price rollouts for solo-developed software.

---

## 1. The Day-One Pricing Axiom: "The $10 / $100 / $1,000 Rule"

On Day One, you do not have data to optimize a price; you have a hypothesis to test. Select the price order of magnitude matching your core audience:

* **~$10 / month (Prosumer / Freelancer)**: High volume, 100% self-serve, automated support (e.g. FreePajak Solo Tier).
* **~$100 / month (SMB / Agency / Team)**: Default B2B SaaS, multi-seat, workflow-critical (e.g. TataBuku Pro, Agency Whiteboard).
* **~$1,000 / month (Mid-Market / Enterprise)**: Business-critical, customized integrations, SLA guarantees.

### The "$9 Trap" Warning
Resist the urge to price at $5–$9/mo (Rp 49k–79k) to artificially boost signups:
* **False Traction**: Attracts price-sensitive customers who consume 80% of support bandwidth, churn the fastest, and resist future upgrades.
* **Economic Trap**: Raising prices 5x–10x later on an anchored customer base is nearly impossible. Start slightly higher with clear value.

## 2. Choosing the Core Value Metric

Your value metric is the primary unit that scales pricing as customer value expands:

| Value Metric Type | Best For | Industry Example | Anti-Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Per Seat / User** | Team collaboration tools | Slack, Notion | Discourages adoption if solo users are primary. |
| **Per Usage / Volume** | Data, API, or infrastructure tools | Twilio, Supabase, AWS | Unpredictable monthly bills scare SMBs without caps. |
| **Per Business Record** | Financial, tax, or CRM systems | FreePajak (Invoices/mo), Mailchimp | Limiting records before user experiences value. |
| **Flat Tiered Fee** | Workflow & utility software | Basecamp | Leaves money on the table as enterprise usage scales. |

*Golden Rule: Ask "When the customer uses more of this metric, do they demonstrably earn more or save more?" If yes, it is a valid value metric.*

## 3. The 3-Tier Packaging Architecture (Good-Better-Best)

1. **Entry Tier ("Good")**:
   - Covers core workflow for solo operators.
   - Guarded by hard usage limits (e.g. max 5 clients, max 50 invoices/month).
2. **Recommended Tier ("Better")**:
   - The primary anchor tier (targeted for 70% of paying users).
   - Unlocks automation, multi-user collaboration, and priority data export.
   - Visually highlighted on pricing page as *"Most Popular / Rekomendasi"*.
3. **Scale Tier ("Best")**:
   - Priced at $2\times - 3\times$ the Better tier.
   - Includes custom integrations, audit logs, and priority SLA support.

## 4. De-Risked Price Increase Rollout Methodology

When increasing prices on an existing product, never flip a global switch overnight:

1. **Test on New Signups First**:
   - Apply the higher price strictly to new incoming signups for 30–60 days. Monitor signup conversion rate. If conversion remains stable, the market has validated the new price point.
2. **Avoid Permanent Grandfathering**:
   - Grandfathering existing users indefinitely creates a multi-year revenue leak. Provide grandfathering as a **time-limited grace window** (e.g. *"Your existing price is locked for 6 months"*).
3. **Staggered Migration Waves**:
   - Roll out the new price to **5%–10% of existing customers first**. Monitor churn and feedback before expanding to 100%.
4. **Value-Add Justification**:
   - Announce price adjustments 60 days in advance by highlighting new features shipped over the past year, paired with an incentive to lock in the old rate via an annual prepayment.
