# Blog Content Strategy Guide

Framework for creating blog content that drives traffic, engagement, and conversion.

---

## 1. Content Strategy Foundation

### Goals (Pick 1-2 Primary)
- **SEO Traffic:** Rank for high-intent keywords → organic signups
- **Thought Leadership:** Build authority in niche → trust & brand awareness
- **Product Education:** Teach users how to use product → activation & retention
- **Community Building:** Create discussion hub → word-of-mouth & retention

### Success Metrics
| Goal | Primary Metric | Secondary Metric |
|------|----------------|------------------|
| SEO Traffic | Organic visitors/month | Keyword rankings (top 10) |
| Thought Leadership | Social shares | Backlinks from authority sites |
| Product Education | Time on page | CTA click rate |
| Community | Comments/replies | Newsletter signups |

  1. 7 Legal Strategies to Save on Freelancer Taxes

## 3. Content Types & Use Cases

### How-to Guides (Evergreen SEO)
**Format:** Step-by-step tutorial with screenshots/examples
**Length:** 1,500-3,000 words
**Example:** "How to Calculate Freelancer PPh 21 in 5 Steps"

**Structure:**
```markdown

**Meta Description:** Learn how to [X] in [Y] simple steps. [Benefit]. [Time estimate]. [Updated 2026].

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Problem statement
- What reader will learn
- Time estimate

## Prerequisites
- [Requirement 1]
- [Requirement 2]

## Step 1: [Action Verb]
[Clear instruction with screenshot/example]

**Common Mistake:** [Pitfall to avoid]

## Step 2: [Action Verb]
...

## Troubleshooting
- **Issue:** [Problem]  
  **Solution:** [Fix]

## Conclusion
- Recap key steps
- CTA: [Try our tool to automate this]

## FAQs
**Q: [Question]**  
A: [Answer]
```

**SEO Tip:** Target long-tail keywords (3-5 words), e.g., "how to calculate freelancer pph 21" not just "tax"

## 4. SEO Optimization Checklist

### Keyword Research (Do This First)
**Tools:** Ahrefs, SEMrush, Ubersuggest, Google Keyword Planner

**Target Keywords:**
- **Primary:** 1 main keyword (search volume 500-5,000/month, difficulty <40)
- **Secondary:** 2-3 related keywords (LSI/semantic variations)
- **Long-tail:** 5-10 question keywords (e.g., "how to do X", "what is the difference between Y")

**Keyword Intent:**
- **Informational:** "what is X" → Top-of-funnel (ToFu)
- **Commercial:** "X vs Y", "best X" → Middle-of-funnel (MoFu)
- **Transactional:** "X pricing", "buy X" → Bottom-of-funnel (BoFu)

**Strategy:** 60% informational, 30% commercial, 10% transactional

---

### On-Page SEO

**Title Tag (60 chars max):**
```
[Keyword] - [Benefit] | [Brand Name]
Example: How to Calculate Freelancer PPh 21 - 2026 Guide | FreePajak
```

**Meta Description (155 chars max):**
```
[Hook] [Keyword] [Benefit] [Call to action] [Year]
Example: Confused about calculating freelancer PPh 21? Follow this complete guide (updated 2026) and legally save on taxes. Free!
```

**URL Slug:**
```
/blog/[keyword-slug]/
Example: /blog/how-to-calculate-freelancer-pph-21/
```

**Heading Structure:**
- H1: 1x (article title)
- H2: 5-8x (main sections)
- H3: 10-15x (subsections)
- Include keyword in H1, 2-3 H2s naturally

**Internal Linking:**
- Link to 3-5 related blog posts
- Link to product/pricing page 1-2x
- Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")

**Images:**
- File name: `keyword-descriptive-name.jpg` (not `IMG_1234.jpg`)
- Alt text: Descriptive sentence with keyword
- Compress: <100KB per image (use TinyPNG)

**Structured Data (Schema.org):**
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Article",
  "headline": "[Title]",
  "author": {
    "@type": "Person",
    "name": "[Author Name]"
  },
  "datePublished": "2026-09-29",
  "image": "[Featured image URL]"
}
```

## 6. Writing Best Practices

### Voice & Tone
**For B2B SaaS (Professional but Approachable):**
- Use "Anda" not "kamu" (formal Indonesian)
- Avoid jargon unless explained
- Short sentences (15-20 words avg)
- Active voice > passive voice

**For B2C / Consumer (Casual & Friendly):**
- Use "kamu" not "Anda"
- Conversational tone (imagine talking to a friend)
- Inject personality (humor, stories, emoji sparingly)

### Readability
- **Flesch Reading Ease:** Target 60-70 (readable by 13-15 year olds)
- **Paragraph length:** 2-4 sentences max
- **Sentence length:** Mix short (5-10 words) and medium (15-20 words)
- **Subheadings:** Every 200-300 words
- **Bullet points:** Use for lists >3 items
- **Bold/italics:** Highlight key points (don't overdo)

### Engagement Hooks
**Opening Hook (First 100 words):**
- Start with surprising stat: "73% of freelancers pay more tax than they should"
- Ask question: "Ever felt confused calculating PPh 21?"
- Tell story: "Last year, I was fined Rp2 million because..."

**CTA (Call to Action):**
- **Soft CTA (educational posts):** "Download free cheat sheet"
- **Medium CTA (comparison posts):** "Try our free calculator"
- **Hard CTA (bottom-funnel posts):** "Start 14-day trial without credit card"

## 7. Content Repurposing (1 Post = 10+ Assets)

**From 1 Blog Post, Create:**
1. **Twitter Thread** (10-15 tweets summarizing key points)
2. **LinkedIn Post** (longer narrative + link to full post)
3. **Instagram Carousel** (5-10 slides with key takeaways)
4. **YouTube Short / TikTok** (60-sec video explaining 1 tip)
5. **Email Newsletter** (excerpt + CTA to read full post)
6. **Podcast Episode** (15-min discussion of topic)
7. **Infographic** (visual summary of listicle)
8. **Lead Magnet** (PDF download with bonus checklist)
9. **Quora Answer** (repurpose sections to answer questions)
10. **Guest Post Pitch** (offer to write similar content for other blogs)

## 9. Content Maintenance (Evergreen Updates)

**Quarterly Refresh Checklist:**
- [ ] Update statistics / data (replace "2025" with "2026")
- [ ] Check broken links (use Screaming Frog)
- [ ] Add new sections for updated regulations / features
- [ ] Improve meta description / title tag (CTR optimization)
- [ ] Add new internal links to recent posts
- [ ] Compress images further if page speed declined

**Sunset Policy:**
- If post gets <10 visitors/month for 6 months → Redirect to updated version or pillar page

## 10. Legal & Compliance (Indonesia Specific)

### Disclosure Requirements
- **Sponsored Content:** Label "Sponsored Content" or "Sponsored" at top
- **Affiliate Links:** Disclose "Affiliate link — we may earn a commission if you purchase"
- **Medical/Legal/Financial Advice Disclaimer:** "This article is for general informational purposes only, not [medical/legal/financial] advice. Consult with a professional before making decisions."

### Copyright
- **Images:** Use only licensed/royalty-free (Unsplash, Pexels, custom)
- **Quotes:** Attribute source, link to original, fair use <300 words
- **Competitor Mentions:** Factual comparisons OK, defamation NOT OK
