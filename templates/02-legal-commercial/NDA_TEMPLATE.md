> **Purpose**: Mutual confidentiality agreement protecting proprietary technology, customer data, and trade secrets between the Client and the Solo Developer prior to sharing source code, databases, or sensitive business workflows.
> **Compliance**: Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP) & Indonesian Civil Code (KUHPerdata).

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary, non-public, technical, commercial, or operational information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, in writing, or in electronic machine-readable form, including but not limited to:
- Source code, software architectures, algorithms, and API specifications.
- Business models, pricing strategies, customer databases, and financial data.
- Personal data and identifiable records subject to statutory privacy under UU PDP No. 27/2022.
- Product roadmaps, unreleased features, and security vulnerability reports.

---

## 3. Obligations of Confidentiality

The Receiving Party agrees to:
1. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, exercising at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own confidential assets of similar nature (and never less than reasonable care).
2. Use Confidential Information strictly for the purpose of evaluating and executing the software development engagement.
3. Restrict disclosure exclusively to authorized personnel who have an absolute need-to-know and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement.
4. Promptly notify the Disclosing Party within **24 hours** upon becoming aware of any unauthorized access, loss, or potential breach involving the Confidential Information.

---

## 4. Exclusions from Confidentiality

Confidential Information does not include information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party.
2. Was already in the rightful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
4. Is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality.

---

## 5. Return and Destruction of Materials

Upon written request by the Disclosing Party or upon termination of the business engagement:
1. The Receiving Party shall promptly return or permanently delete all copies of Confidential Information stored on local workstations, staging servers, or development databases.
2. If personal data is involved, data deletion and anonymization must comply with Article 8 of Law No. 27 of 2022 (UU PDP).

---

## 6. Term and Termination

1. This Agreement takes effect immediately upon signature and remains in effect for a period of **2 (two) years** from the date of final disclosure.
2. Obligations regarding trade secrets, source code IP, and statutory personal data survive the termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

---

## 7. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

1. This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.
2. Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall first be resolved through good-faith amicable negotiation within 30 calendar days, failing which the dispute shall be submitted to the jurisdiction of the District Court of [Jakarta / Client Domicile].
