## 2. The 3 Value Pillars (Core Value Proposition)

Define the 3 distinct pillars that justify why a customer should pay for this product over competitors:

### Pillar 1: [Speed / Efficiency Outcome]
* **Headline**: `[e.g., Dari 15 Jam Menjadi 3 Menit]`
* **Customer Benefit**: `[Explain how much time or manual frustration is eliminated.]`
* **Proof Point (Reason to Believe)**: `[Concrete mechanism: e.g. 1-click bank mutation sync, automated invoice parsing.]`

### Pillar 2: [Compliance / Risk Protection Outcome]
* **Headline**: `[e.g., 100% Sesuai Aturan Pajak PP 55/2022]`
* **Customer Benefit**: `[Eliminates fear of government audit, penalty fines, or legal risk.]`
* **Proof Point (Reason to Believe)**: `[Pre-built progressive tax brackets (PPh 21/Final), verified by certified tax consultants.]`

### Pillar 3: [Simplicity / Developer-Grade Design Outcome]
* **Headline**: `[e.g., Tanpa Setup Rumit, Langsung Pakai]`
* **Customer Benefit**: `[Zero learning curve, works out of the box without onboarding calls.]`
* **Proof Point (Reason to Believe)**: `[Google OAuth login, zero database setup, export directly to CSV / DJP Online format.]`

## 3. Audience-Segmented Pitch Matrix

Tailor the value message depending on which persona is viewing the product:

| Persona Segment | Primary Pain Point | Tailored Hook / Pitch | Primary Call-to-Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solo Operator / Freelancer** | Confused by complicated tax formulas; afraid of DJP fines. | *"Fokus ngoding dan cari klien, urusan hitung PPh 21 biar kami yang bereskan otomatis."* | `[Mulai Hitung Gratis]` |
| **Small Agency Owner** | Spending 20+ hours every month reconciling client invoices. | *"Kelola invoice multi-klien dan bukti potong dalam satu dashboard terpusat."* | `[Coba Demo 14 Hari]` |
| **Finance / Accountant** | Manual Excel formulas break easily and lack audit logs. | *"Format ekspor baku CSV yang siap diimpor langsung ke e-Faktur dan e-Bupot."* | `[Lihat Integrasi]` |
