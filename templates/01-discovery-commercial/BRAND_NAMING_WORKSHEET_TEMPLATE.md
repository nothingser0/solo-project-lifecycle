## 5. Banned Name Shapes (auto-reject)

- Overused suffixes unless strategically justified: `-ly`, `-ify`, `-io`, `-hub`, `-labs`, `-works`
- One letter away from a competitor's name
- Unintended meaning in Bahasa Indonesia or the relevant regional language
- Locks the brand to one product when expansion is likely
- Sounds like a cheap SaaS tool when the brand is not one

## 7. Pre-Finalisation Checklist

- [ ] Domain available (`.com` + `.id` for Indonesian brands)
- [ ] Trademark search in the category and market — **done by a professional, not the agent**
- [ ] Social handles available
- [ ] Said aloud; feels natural
- [ ] Spelled correctly by someone who heard it once
- [ ] No unintended meaning in the target market's language
- [ ] Registered as a wordmark (recommended before launch, not after)

> The agent can shortlist and strategise. Trademark clearance and registration are outside agent authority and outside agent competence.
