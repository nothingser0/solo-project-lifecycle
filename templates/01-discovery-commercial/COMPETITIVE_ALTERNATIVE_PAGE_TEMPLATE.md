# Competitive Comparison & Alternative Page Template

> **Module**: M00 (Product Discovery) & M04 (UI/UX Landing Page Design)  
> **Source**: Adapted from high-converting SaaS competitor positioning frameworks (`coreyhaines31/marketingskills`).  
> **Objective**: Capture high-intent organic search traffic (e.g., "[Competitor] alternative") and provide transparent, balanced evaluations that convert switcher traffic without dishonest claims.

---

## 1. Page Header & SEO Metadata

* **Target URL Pattern**: `/[competitor]-alternative` or `/compare/[your-product]-vs-[competitor]`
* **Page Title (H1)**: `[Your Product]: A Better [Competitor] Alternative for [Target Audience]`
* **Meta Description**: `Looking for a simpler, faster alternative to [Competitor]? See how [Your Product] compares on pricing, key features, and ease of use.`
* **Primary Target Keywords**: `[Competitor] alternative`, `best [Competitor] alternatives`, `[Your Product] vs [Competitor]`.

---

## 2. The 3-Sentence TL;DR Executive Summary

Place immediately above the fold for scanning visitors:

> **The Quick Take**: While `[Competitor]` is built for `[Competitor's core demographic, e.g., large enterprise teams with dedicated finance departments]`, `[Your Product]` is laser-focused on `[Your target demographic, e.g., solo freelancers and small agencies]`. With `[Your Product]`, you get `[Core primary differentiator, e.g., automated PPh 21 calculation and 1-click WhatsApp invoicing]` at a transparent `[Your price point]` without annual enterprise contracts.

## 4. Honest Positioning: "Who Should Choose Whom"

Honest differentiation builds trust with evaluators:

### When to Choose [Competitor Name]
Be completely transparent about scenarios where the competitor is the superior fit:
* *You require complex multi-subsidiary enterprise consolidation.*
* *You have a dedicated in-house IT team to maintain custom ERP integrations.*
* *You need legacy on-premise server deployment.*

### When to Choose [Your Product Name]
* *You want an intuitive tool that requires zero staff training.*
* *You need native compliance with Indonesian tax/payment infrastructure without third-party plugins.*
* *You want predictable monthly pricing without mandatory sales onboarding fees.*

---

## 5. Frictionless Data Migration Path

Alleviate the fear of switching data platforms:
1. **Export from Competitor**: Step-by-step guidance on downloading CSV/JSON exports from `[Competitor]`.
2. **1-Click Import Tool**: Built-in mapping wizard that parses competitor CSV layouts automatically.
3. **Migration Assurance**: *"Need help migrating your historical records? Our team will import your data for free within 24 hours."*
