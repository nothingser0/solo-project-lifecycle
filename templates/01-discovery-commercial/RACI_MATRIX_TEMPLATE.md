## RACI Legend

- **R = Responsible**: The person(s) who do the work to complete the task
- **A = Accountable**: The person with final approval authority (only ONE per deliverable)
- **C = Consulted**: People whose input is sought (two-way communication)
- **I = Informed**: People who are kept up-to-date (one-way communication)

**Golden Rules**:
1. Each deliverable has exactly ONE "A" (Accountable) - the decision maker
2. Multiple people can be "R" (Responsible) for collaborative work
3. Minimize "C" entries - too many cooks spoil the broth
4. Use "I" liberally for transparency without slowing down execution

## RACI Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### ❌ Too Many Accountables
**Problem**: Deliverable has 2+ "A" entries → Decision paralysis, finger-pointing  
**Fix**: ONE person is accountable. If co-decision needed, escalate to higher authority.

### ❌ Responsible Without Authority
**Problem**: Solo Dev is "R" but needs approval from 5 "C" stakeholders → Bottleneck  
**Fix**: Limit "C" to max 2-3 critical voices. Others move to "I".

### ❌ Informed Overload
**Problem**: Everyone is "I" on everything → Inbox fatigue, ignored updates  
**Fix**: Only inform stakeholders who need to act on the information.

### ❌ No Responsible Party
**Problem**: Deliverable has "A" and "C" but no "R" → Work doesn't get done  
**Fix**: Assign clear owner to execute the task.

### ❌ Accountable But No Authority
**Problem**: Junior dev is "A" but can't approve budget or override senior stakeholder  
**Fix**: Match authority level with accountability level.

## RACI Review Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- [ ] Every deliverable has exactly ONE "A" (Accountable)
- [ ] Every deliverable has at least ONE "R" (Responsible)
- [ ] No deliverable has >3 "C" (Consulted) entries
- [ ] "I" (Informed) is used sparingly for essential updates only
- [ ] Decision authority matches organizational hierarchy
- [ ] Solo dev context: "A" defaults to Product Owner or Client PIC
- [ ] Company context: "A" aligns with role authority (CTO for tech, CFO for budget)
- [ ] Conflict resolution paths are clear
- [ ] Backup authorities defined for each critical "A" role

## Notes & Customization

**Solo Developer Context**:
- Collapse multiple "R" entries into Solo Dev + Client PIC
- "A" typically = Client PIC (approval) or Solo Dev (execution decision)
- QA/Designer/DevOps may be external contractors or same person as Solo Dev

**Company/Team Context**:
- Full separation of concerns (Dev, QA, DevOps, Design)
- CTO = technical authority, Product Owner = scope authority, CFO = budget authority
- Legal + Compliance involved in regulated industries (fintech, healthcare)
