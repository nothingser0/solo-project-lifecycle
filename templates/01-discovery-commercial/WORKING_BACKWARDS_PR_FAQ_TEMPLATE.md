# Amazon "Working Backwards" PR/FAQ Template

> **Module**: M00 (Product Discovery & Strategy) / M01 (Idea Feasibility)  
> **Archetype**: Solo SaaS / Independent Product / MVP  
> **Philosophy**: Write the press release and customer FAQ *before* writing a single line of spec or code. If the announcement isn't compelling, building it won't fix it.

---

## Part 1: The Press Release (One Page Max)

### Heading
**[City, Date] — [Product Name] Launches to Help [Target Audience] [Core Value Proposition / Outcome]**

### Subheading
*[One sentence summarizing the benefit and who it is for]*

### Summary Paragraph
[Company / Project Name] today announced the public release of **[Product Name]**, a [category/type of solution] designed for [target audience]. Starting today, [target users] can [primary capability/outcome without jargon], eliminating [the single biggest pain point they face today].

### Problem Paragraph
Historically, [target persona] had to deal with [status quo pain point]. Existing alternatives either [fail mode 1: e.g. too expensive/complex] or [fail mode 2: e.g. manual spreadsheets prone to errors]. As a result, [quantified consequence: e.g. wasting 15 hours every month / risking compliance fines].

### Solution Paragraph
**[Product Name]** solves this by [how the product works in simple terms]. With [Product Name], users can:
- **[Benefit 1]**: [Actionable outcome]
- **[Benefit 2]**: [Actionable outcome]
- **[Benefit 3]**: [Actionable outcome]

### Customer Quote (Hypothetical Ideal Testimonial)
> "[Product Name] completely changed how we handle [problem]. Before, I spent [frustration]. Now, it takes [time/cost saved], and I never have to worry about [risk] again."  
> — **[Customer Name]**, [Role/Company]

### Founder / Creator Quote
> "We built [Product Name] because solo operators and small teams shouldn't have to choose between clunky enterprise tools and broken manual workflows. Our goal was to make [outcome] seamless, fast, and accessible."  
> — **[Your Name]**, Creator of [Product Name]

### Call to Action & Getting Started
[Product Name] is available starting today at `[https://yourproduct.com]`. New users can [pricing/onboarding action: e.g. start a free 14-day trial / use the free tier / access instant demo without credit card].

## Part 2: Customer FAQ (External Questions)

### Q1: Who is this product built for?
**A**: [Define precise user profile, business size, and required prerequisites. Also state who this is NOT for.]

### Q2: How is this different from [Competitor A] or [Status Quo / Excel]?
**A**: [Highlight the distinct unfair advantage: speed, localized compliance, zero setup, pricing transparency, or workflow focus.]

### Q3: How much does it cost?
**A**: [Clear, transparent pricing structure. E.g., Free tier limits + Paid tier price in IDR / USD without hidden fees.]

### Q4: How difficult is the onboarding process?
**A**: [Time to First Value (TTFV): e.g. "Under 3 minutes. Sign up with Google, import your CSV, and generate your first report immediately."]

### Q5: How is my data protected?
**A**: [Data security & compliance highlights: encryption at rest, UU PDP compliance, zero unauthorized data selling.]

## Part 3: Internal Reality Check FAQ (Solo Dev Pre-Mortem)

### Q1: What is the single hardest technical obstacle in building this?
**A**: [State the bottleneck honestly: e.g. third-party webhook reliability, background PDF render limits, or scraping durability.]

### Q2: What is the primary distribution channel to get the first 100 paying users?
**A**: [Cold outreach, SEO, niche community, Twitter/LinkedIn, or existing network. "Hope people find it" is not an answer.]

### Q3: If this project fails in 6 months, what was the most likely reason?
**A**: [Name the mortal risk: customer willingness to pay was zero, customer churn was too high, or distribution channel was choked.]

### Q4: What is the absolute minimum scope required to test this press release?
**A**: [The razor-thin MVP that validates the core customer quote above, deferring all secondary nice-to-haves.]
